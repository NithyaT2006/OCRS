About

An Online Complaint Registration and Management System is a digital platform designed to make grievance handling simple, transparent, and efficient. It allows citizens, customers, or employees to register complaints online and track their resolution in real time.

Benefits

  • Builds trust through transparent processes.
  • Improves efficiency in complaint handling.
  • Provides valuable data for administrators to analyze and improve services
